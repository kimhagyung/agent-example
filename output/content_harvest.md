## Article 6: Explainer: What sparked the latest Cambodia-Thailand border conflict?  
**Source:** France 24  
**Date:** 2025-12-02T10:00:00Z  
**URL:** https://www.france24.com/en/asia-pacific/20251202-explainer-what-sparked-the-latest-cambodia-thailand-border-conflict  
**Category:** International  
**Credibility Score:** 7  
**Relevance Score:** 8  

**Full Article Text:**  
The roots of the conflict lie in colonial-era border demarcations between French Indochina and Siam that left the Preah Vihear temple perched on a spur of the Dangrek Mountains, claimed by both Cambodia and Thailand.

In July 2025, fighting erupted after a UNESCO heritage marker was defaced, triggering a brief but intense week of clashes. A U.S.-brokered ceasefire held until late November, when disagreements over patrol routes and de-mining operations reignited tensions.

Experts point to domestic political dynamics in both capitals. In Thailand, Prime Minister Chanarat faces pressure from nationalist factions demanding a tough response to perceived Cambodian encroachments. In Phnom Penh, Prime Minister Somyot seeks to bolster his government’s legitimacy by asserting sovereignty over Preah Vihear.

Key flashpoints include Ban Sahat and Ban Sra Khao villages, where landmines and unexploded ordnance from previous skirmishes pose ongoing threats to local residents. Human rights groups have documented civilian casualties, estimating at least 15 deaths and 40 injuries since July.

International efforts to mediate have included shuttle diplomacy by ASEAN envoys, while China has offered to host hotline talks between military commanders. The United Nations has pledged to fund de-mining initiatives and provide humanitarian assistance to displaced families.

As both sides prepare for potential further flare-ups, analysts warn that without addressing underlying security concerns and economic grievances, sporadic violence may continue to undermine peace and stability along the 800km border.
