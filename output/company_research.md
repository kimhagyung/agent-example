## Company Overview  
**Leaseweb** is a global Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) provider headquartered in Amsterdam, founded in 1997.  
- **Size:** ~501–1,000 employees worldwide  
- **Customers:** ~20,000 organizations (SMBs to enterprises)  
- **Network footprint:** 28 data centers across Europe, Asia, Australia, and North America; >80,000 servers; 10 Tbps+ network capacity  
- **Core offerings:** Dedicated servers, public & private cloud, colocation, CDN, cybersecurity, network services  

## Mission and Values  
**Mission:** To empower businesses with highly reliable, scalable, and customizable infrastructure, backed by exceptional customer service and technical support.  
**Values:**  
- *Reliability & Uptime* – 99.999% SLAs, geo-redundant architectures  
- *Customer Centricity* – 24/7 support, flexible SLAs, self-service API  
- *Innovation* – continuous investment in automation, platform tools, new markets  
- *Sustainability* – green data centers (hydro-powered in Quebec), energy efficiency  

## Recent News or Changes  
- **Aug 15, 2024:** Announced major U.S. East Coast expansion—new/expanded data centers in Washington D.C., New York City, and Miami, adding 115+ racks and bolstering geo-redundant services for colocation, cloud, and dedicated servers.  
- **2021–2023:** Acquired and rebranded iWeb into Leaseweb Canada; launched new “Network Connectivity” and “Managed Kubernetes” products.  
- **Ongoing:** Sustainability push with ISO 27001, SOC 1/2, PCI DSS certifications across 28 global sites.  

## Role Context and Product Involvement  
As a **Senior Golang Developer** on the **Developer Platform Team**, you will:  
- Design, implement, and maintain **Go-based microservices** and internal APIs powering Leaseweb’s self-service portal and automation pipelines.  
- Collaborate with Infrastructure, Network, and Security teams to integrate provisioning (bare-metal & virtual), billing, IPAM, and monitoring tools.  
- Own CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or GitLab CI), containerization (Docker), and orchestration (Kubernetes).  
- Shape platform SDKs/CLIs and developer-facing tooling to streamline resource onboarding and configuration.  
- Participate in on-call rotations and incident response for platform reliability.  

_Typical team size: 8–12 engineers, Agile sprint cadence, global stakeholders (EU/U.S./APAC)._  

## Likely Interview Topics  
- **Golang Fundamentals:** goroutines, channels, sync primitives, error handling, profiling/benchmarking  
- **System & API Design:** microservices architecture, REST/gRPC design, rate-limiting, retries, circuit breakers  
- **Infrastructure as Code:** Terraform modules, state management, drift detection  
- **Cloud/IaaS Concepts:** bare-metal provisioning workflows, virtualization vs. containerization, network overlays  
- **CI/CD & DevOps:** pipeline authoring, container builds, automated testing, deployment strategies (blue/green, canary)  
- **Testing & Quality:** unit tests, integration tests, mocks in Go, code coverage  
- **Reliability & Security:** observability (Prometheus, Grafana), alerting, secrets management  
- **Collaboration & Process:** Agile rituals, code reviews, documentation standards, cross-team communication
