# Interview Prep: Leaseweb – Senior Golang Developer - Developer Platform Team

## Job Overview
Leaseweb seeks a **Senior Golang Developer** to join its **Developer Platform** team in **Amsterdam**. The role requires close collaboration with Infrastructure, Network, and Security teams, ownership of CI/CD pipelines, containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), and participation in on-call rotations to ensure platform reliability.

## Predicted Interview Questions
1. **Golang Fundamentals**  
   – Explain goroutines vs. OS threads and how you’d coordinate them with channels.  
   – How do you handle errors idiomatically in Go?  
   – Describe your approach to profiling and benchmarking Go services.
2. **System & API Design**  
   – Design a microservice architecture for provisioning bare-metal servers with rate limiting, retries, and circuit breakers.  
   – How would you expose internal services via REST vs. gRPC in terms of performance and versioning?
3. **Infrastructure & DevOps**  
   – Outline how you’d build a reusable Terraform module for provisioning Kubernetes clusters.  
   – Walk me through your CI/CD pipeline design for blue/green or canary deployments.
4. **Cloud/IaaS Concepts**  
   – Compare bare-metal provisioning workflows with virtual machine orchestration.  
   – How do you ensure network segmentation and security for multi-tenant cloud services?
5. **Reliability & Observability**  
   – What metrics and alerts would you set up in Prometheus/Grafana for a Go-based platform service?  
   – Describe an incident you’ve handled; how did you troubleshoot and communicate status to stakeholders?
6. **Collaboration & Process**  
   – How do you conduct effective code reviews in a distributed team?  
   – Give an example of streamlining developer onboarding or reducing deployment friction.

## Strategic Advice
- **Tone & Focus**: Be confident about your full-stack and platform achievements; emphasize your developer-­experience mindset and automation wins.  
- **Addressing Golang Gap**: Acknowledge limited Go experience but highlight fast ramp-up in new languages and reference self-study plans (e.g., Go modules, Go by Example, Exercism Go tracks).  
- **Relocation**: Reinforce your readiness to relocate immediately and integrate on-site for better collaboration.

  • _Lack of direct Go background_: Mitigate by sharing your hands-on microservice and DevOps successes and eagerness to apply those learnings in Go.  
  • _On-site requirement_: Emphasize your commitment to Amsterdam relocation and cross-regional experience.  
- **Closing the Loop**: End with a strong “fit statement”—tie your automation, reliability focus, and developer platform passion directly to Leaseweb’s mission of empowering customers with scalable, reliable infrastructure.
